What is Pregnancy Risk

A high - risk pregnancy is one where either the mother or baby has a higher chance of health problems
in early delivery. Several factors can contribute to a high - risk pregnancy , including pre-existing health
conditions in the mother,complications from previous pregnancies, Age, Miscarriage and lifestyle choices.

Pre-existing Medical Conditions: like high blood pressure, diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease,
autoimmune disorders,and infections can pose risks during pregnancy.

Maternal Age: Women under 20 or over 35 are considered at higher risk.

Previous Pregnancy Complications: Women with a history of preterm labor, preeclampsia, or other complications
in previous pregnancies are at higher risk.

Multiple Gestation: Carrying twins, triplets, or more increases the risk of complications.

Lifestyle Choices: Smoking, excessive alcohol consumption, and drug use can negatively impact pregnancy.

Obesity: Having a high BMI can also increase the risk of complications.
